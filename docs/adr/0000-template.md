# NNNN. Short title in the imperative

* Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
* Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play — technical, operational, organisational? State the problem, not the
solution. Include the constraints that rule options in or out.

## Decision

What we decided, in active voice: "We will …". Name the alternatives considered and why they
lost.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what follow-up work this creates. Include the
migration impact on existing deployments.
